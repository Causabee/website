Purchases

One purchase counts on all your devices, if each is signed in with the same Apple Account.

In Causabee, open Settings, then Plan and usage (on the Mac: Plan). Choose Restore Purchases. The same button is there when you change plan.

Plans and credits

What do Free, Plus and Pro hold?

  • Free holds 3 open matters. A closed matter doesn’t count. Apple’s model on your device reads what comes in. Nothing of it leaves your device.
  • Plus holds every matter. It reads the same way as Free.
  • Pro holds every matter and 3,000 credits a month. A bigger AI reads what comes in, writes each matter’s brief and answers in the chat. Names are disguised first.

You see the price in the App Store, in your own currency.

What is a credit?

Pro’s work is counted in credits. After each piece of work, Causabee says how many credits it used. A top-up adds 1,000 credits, kept until you use them.

What is the free top-up at the start?

A new Causabee gets one top-up for free: 1,000 credits, once for each Apple Account. At its first start, the app asks Causabee’s server for it by itself, with Apple’s signed record of the download and a random id the app made. Nothing of a matter goes, and no name.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Your plan and credits belong to your Apple Account and to a random id the app makes by itself. There is no sign-up and no name.

Your matters on other devices

Matters are missing on another device

Matters move between your devices through your own iCloud, not through Causabee’s server. Each device needs the same Apple Account.

iCloud is on from the first start, on every device that is signed in to iCloud. A device that is not signed in shows a line that says so, and its matters stay on it until you sign in.

On the Mac, open Settings, under iCloud, to see whether it is On. If you switch it to Off, that takes effect when Causabee starts again. Off keeps every matter on the Mac and takes nothing out of iCloud. A line can also say that iCloud is waiting, because a copy of your matters needs more room first.

On the iPhone and iPad there is no switch in Causabee. Check that the device is signed in to iCloud, and that iCloud is allowed for Causabee in the device’s own Settings.

Do I need a mail account?

No. A mail account is optional. Mail is one source among others: screenshots, photos, files and scans, notes, the chat, and tasks and dates added by hand work without any account.

What goes to OpenAI

What goes to OpenAI, and what never does?

Only with Pro, or a top-up. Then what comes in goes to OpenAI as text, through Causabee’s own server, whatever its source.

Before it goes, names, addresses and numbers are replaced on your device first. About 98 % of them are found. A check holds back a text that still has a name Causabee knows.

Causabee’s server passes the request on and sends the answer back. It keeps no text, only how much each request used.

Pictures and files themselves are never sent. Their text is read on your device.

Without Pro, Apple’s model on your device does the reading. Nothing of what comes in leaves your device.

The whole story: the privacy policy

Your account and data

Can I take my matters with me?

Yes, at any time. In a matter’s menu (…), choose Export as RTF. The whole matter becomes one text document, which Pages, Word and TextEdit open.

Where are the files that came in?

In your iCloud Drive, in the folder Causabee, with one folder for each matter. Files and scans, photos, screenshots and attachments are all there. Open them in the Files app on iPhone and iPad, or in Finder on the Mac, without Causabee. iCloud Drive must be on for Causabee.

The texts that came in, and the chat, stay in your private iCloud. What Causabee found in them is in the export: tasks, dates, people, details, notes, links and the history. The export goes into the matter’s folder too.

How do I delete my account and my data?

What is on your own devices and in your iCloud, you delete yourself. The privacy policy says where, under “Your choices”.

The server makes an account when you get the free top-up, or buy Pro or a top-up. Support can remove it. Write to support to ask. Support tells you what it needs to find it.

What the server keeps is a record of use and purchases: when, which model, how many tokens, what it cost. No text and no name. Part of it stays as long as the law requires the developer to keep accounts. The whole list is in the privacy policy, under “Causabee Pro and its server”.
